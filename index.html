Baby Monitor

Works entirely on your device. Watches his face and alarms if it gets covered or disappears.

Camera off

  1. Start the camera and aim it at his face.
  2. Tap or drag on the video to put the green box over his face, and resize it so it fits just his face.
  3. Press Calibrate while his face is clearly visible and uncovered.
  4. Press Start monitoring.

Event log

    This is a simple colour and texture check, not artificial intelligence. It can only tell that the face area no longer looks like the calibration. It cannot detect choking, vomiting or breathing, and it can be fooled by blankets that look like skin, by big lighting changes, or if he turns his head away. Re-calibrate if you move the camera or the lights change. It is a helper, not a medical device, and no substitute for a certified monitor or for checking on your baby. Keep the screen on and the device plugged in. If your baby may be choking or not breathing, act immediately and call emergency services.
